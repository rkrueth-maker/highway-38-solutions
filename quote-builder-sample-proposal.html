Representative assumptions

  • Field dimensions and existing conditions must be verified before construction issue.
  • Customer selections are completed before ordering or final trade pricing.
  • Permit, code, utility, engineering, and licensed-trade requirements are confirmed by the responsible professionals.
  • Concealed conditions, hazardous materials, capacity deficiencies, and unverified site conditions are excluded until documented.

Representative exclusions

  • This document is not a contract, accepted price, permit drawing, or construction authorization.
  • Internal labor cost, margin, vendor pricing, subcontractor comparisons, and approval history are not shown.
  • No customer send, purchase, payment, scheduling, or work start occurs automatically.
  • Changes require a documented scope and pricing review before approval.

Customer copy and acceptance controls

A real approved proposal can be printed or saved as a PDF so the customer has an exact copy of the accepted scope and revision. Drafts and public demonstrations should always be visibly marked, and protected internal fields must never appear in the customer output.
