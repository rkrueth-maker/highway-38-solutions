# Office Public UQB Demo Release Trigger

This release marker intentionally triggers the existing **Deploy Unified Owner Portal** workflow after the Office-driven public demo workflow is already present on `main`.

The successful deployment then triggers **Office Public UQB Demo**, which creates and verifies the persistent H38 Business Office demonstration records and files:

- 1 ground-up new-house project beginning with lot clearing
- 14 complete phase quotes
- 56 itemized quote lines
- 84 scope sections
- 10 CAD drawing records and Drive files
- 14 quote PDFs and 1 combined package
- Sanitized public quote and CAD rendering

No new Apps Script project or deployment is created. No quote send, purchase, payment, scheduling, approval, or work authorization occurs automatically.
