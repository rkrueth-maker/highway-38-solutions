Universal Quote Builder overview

Complete quote examples matched to their CAD drawings

The Universal Quote Builder keeps scope, itemized pricing, assumptions, completion requirements, and coordinated drawings together. These public examples are shown here—directly beneath the house example—rather than on a separate result board.

Complete quoteCustomer-facing scope and itemized pricing are shown together instead of as disconnected notes.
Matched CADEach quote example is paired with the drawing sheets that directly support its scope.
Portable packageOpen the quote, inspect the original full-size CAD sheets, or print and save the complete matched package.
Public examples only: This library uses a standalone fictional demonstration dataset and public CAD files stored with the website. It does not connect to, read, or expose private Highway 38 customer, pricing, margin, vendor, user, approval, document, or log records.
Demonstration control: These examples are fictional and are not contracts, permit sets, stamped designs, or construction authorizations. Actual work requires verified site information, current pricing, applicable licensed-professional review, authority approval, and authorized release.
