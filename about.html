About Highway 38

Built by a machinist. Run for the people still doing the work.

Highway 38 Solutions is Rick Krueth’s company. Rick spent his career in machine shops — CNC programming, setup, and supervision — until a bad back and fibromyalgia ended his time on the floor. So he built the tool he wished he’d had: one office that runs the paperwork side, so the hours go back to the work.

The founder

From the machine floor to the Business Office.

Rick was a machinist by trade — programming and setting up CNC mills and lathes, designing fixtures and tooling, tending robots, quoting parts, and running production. Machine shops are his home ground.

His body made the call his head wouldn’t: a bad back and fibromyalgia closed the shop door. What didn’t close was the knowledge — how quoting really works, how jobs pile up, and how the paperwork eats an owner’s evenings.

He knows the contracting field well, and the Office is built for the people still in it: one system for customers, quotes, jobs, schedule, and money — AI help that drafts, and a person who always approves.
