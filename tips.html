Facts

What paperwork really costs

15.4 hours a week

Owners of small HVAC shops (1–10 people) spend an average of 15.4 hours a week on paperwork — calls, estimates, invoices, scheduling, follow-ups. That’s two full workdays not turning a wrench. (Source: WorkHero / HVAC School survey)

A full day a week

45% of small business owners spend roughly one day a week or more on administrative issues. (Source: Oasis, survey of ~300 businesses)

22 working days a year

22 working days a year — just on cash admin: counting it, driving it to the bank. That’s about 178 hours a year. (Source: Post Office study of 500 owners)

Count yours honestly

Quoting. Paperwork. Payroll. How much of your week goes to work that doesn’t pay you? An AI office worker can draft the quote, file the paperwork, and chase the follow-up — so those hours turn into jobs instead.
