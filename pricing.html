AI phone & texting

Your phone. Your number. Yours.

Setup: $99 one-time — free for the founding 10. AI phone and texting is own-it, on purpose: you own your carrier account and your number, and we never run your phone account for you. We set it up with you — routing, greeting and FAQs, forwarding or porting, and a live test call — then you approve what the AI may say and send. It stays off until you turn it on.

After setup, usage is the provider pass-through in the table above: about $1/mo for the number, texts at about 0.4¢–0.83¢ each, and answered calls at about 1¢/min carrier plus about 5¢/min while the AI is talking. No markup, ever.

Fine print, in plain language

No surprises.

Crew size: flat pricing covers your whole crew — office and field, no per-user fees. Running a bigger operation? We'll put together a custom quote.

Texting: if you want the Office to send texts (reminders, on-my-way, follow-ups), the phone carriers charge per message. We pass that cost straight through with no markup — we'll tell you exactly what it is before you turn it on.

AI usage: the plan includes 5,000 actions per month (drafted quotes, follow-ups, review requests, digests). Most businesses never come close. Need more? Add 5,000 extra actions for $49.99.

Payments: the Office does not process credit cards yet. Your customers pay however you accept payment — Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, or bank transfer — and the Office records it against the invoice. Online card processing is planned but not available yet.

Payroll: calculates hours, prepares payroll, and exports clean reports. It does not file taxes or move money — you or your accountant handle that.

Customer-facing features: the customer portal, online booking, automated review requests, and on-my-way texts are optional and off by default. Each has its own toggle in Owner Controls; nothing reaches your customers until you switch it on.

AI: assists, never gates. Every workflow works manually, and anything the AI drafts needs your approval before it goes out.
