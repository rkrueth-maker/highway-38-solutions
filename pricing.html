H38 AI pricing policy

AI is built in—not sold as a separate public plan.

H38 AI is built into all three Highway 38 software products.

Normal product use is included within the selected product and approved scope. High-volume processing, advanced integrations, custom agents, and specialized automation are quoted only when the work requires them.

No product is described as offering unlimited AI, storage, support, integrations, customization, or processing.

Payment terms

Clear approval points before work and launch.

Quote Builder

Monthly subscription. Assisted setup, when selected, is paid before setup work begins.

Business Office

$2,500 implementation is paid 50% before work and 50% before production launch; the $249 monthly subscription begins according to the approved launch terms.

Configured Business System

Projects starting at $7,500 use written milestones. A typical structure is 50% before work, 25% at configuration review, and 25% before production launch.
