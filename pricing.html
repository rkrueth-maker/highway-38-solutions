H38 AI pricing policy

AI is built in—not sold as a separate public plan.

H38 AI is built into every Highway 38 product. Capabilities and usage allowances increase with each product level.

Normal use is included. High-volume image or document processing, large OCR workloads, advanced site measurement, custom AI agents, external integrations, and specialized automation are handled through the appropriate product level, implementation scope, approved change order, or custom quote.

No product is described as offering unlimited AI, storage, support, integrations, or processing.
