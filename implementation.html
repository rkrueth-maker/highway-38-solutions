Protected launch rule

No customer message, approval, scheduling commitment, purchase, payment movement, payroll action, tax filing, or production deployment is treated as automatic unless that exact workflow is separately approved and verified.

Four controlled phases

A documented path from current workflow to accepted production use.

Phase 1

Discover and preserve

Confirm the business, users, current tools, required records, workflow, risks, approval owners, data sources, existing URLs, and anything that must not be replaced or lost.

Phase 2

Configure and migrate

Set branding, company settings, users, roles, templates, price-book items, forms, folders, workflow rules, controlled integrations, and approved data imports.

Phase 3

Verify and train

Run structural checks, sample records, role checks, mobile and desktop checks, save-and-reopen tests, document/PDF tests, backup checks, and staff walkthroughs.

Phase 4

Launch and hand off

Move the approved configuration into production, preserve rollback evidence, confirm the live deployment, document owner controls, and identify the next review point.

Acceptance evidence

A launch is complete only when the exact production system is checked.

Functional evidence

Sign-in, role access, record creation, save and reopen, edits, attachments, PDFs, approvals, logs, backups, and controlled-action behavior.

Deployment evidence

Exact source commit, deployment run, production URL, preserved deployment identifiers, rollback reference, and live verification.

Owner acceptance

Technical checks do not replace the owner’s review of appearance, workflow fit, wording, permissions, and readiness for real business use.
