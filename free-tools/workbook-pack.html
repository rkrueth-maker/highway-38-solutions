TRADES WORKBOOK PACK
By Highway 38 Solutions • Job costing, quotes, mileage & expenses — in one workbook
What this isFive working sheets for a small trades business. No software to learn, no subscription. Fill in the yellow cells — everything else calculates.
Start in this order1) Price Book — put in your real prices. 2) Job Costing — check a job makes money before you quote it. 3) Quote Builder — send the customer a Good / Better / Best quote. 4) Mileage Log — log every business drive. 5) Expense Tracker — keep receipts in one place for tax time.
Yellow cellsYellow = you type here. White / grey cells are formulas — don't type over them, or that calculation will stop working.
Example dataEach sheet has ONE example row in italics so you can see how it works. Delete or overwrite the example before you use the sheet for real.
Tax noteMileage uses the rate you enter on that sheet. Enter the current IRS standard mileage rate for the year you're logging — check irs.gov for the current rate. This workbook helps you keep records; it is not tax advice.
Your numbers onlyEvery price, rate and cost in the examples is a placeholder. Replace them with your shop's real numbers before quoting a customer.
The side notesEach sheet carries a short grey note marked “With the Office:”. This workbook works by hand, start to finish — nothing here needs the Office. The notes just show what the Office does automatically at that same step, so you can see which chores it would take off your plate.
Blue notesBlue highlighted lines are instructions — they walk you through each sheet.
Sheets: Job Costing • Quote Builder (Good–Better–Best) • Mileage Log • Expense Tracker • Price Book
PrintingTo print a quote or invoice with your own logo on it: fill in the Your Business sheet once, then open Print Quote or Print Invoice — they fill in from Quote Builder and print on one clean page.
Your BusinessYour business name, contact details, payment terms and the logo spot live on the Your Business sheet. Your printed quotes and invoices carry your details and your logo — that sheet has the paste-your-logo steps.
