Basis (northern Minnesota, 2026): plowing runs a $45 call-out plus 2.0–4.5¢ per sq ft per visit; mowing a $35 call-out plus 0.12–0.28¢ per sq ft. A season contract is priced off the per-visit midpoint times a typical season — about 18 plowing events or 22 mowing visits — and quoted here within ±10%. Long, steep, gravel, or obstacle-heavy properties price above the range; small flat ones below it. This is an estimate for planning, not a quote.
