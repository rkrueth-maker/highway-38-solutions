Ridge

“There’s a solution for that. Let’s get the right people on it.”

Ridge runs point for the whole company. Bring him any problem — software, a setup, a part that needs making — and he frames it, then hands it to the right specialist. The first voice you meet, and the one who makes sure nothing gets dropped.

The Office

Hank

“I’ll draft it — you approve it.”

Hank is the AI office worker inside the Business Office. He drafts quotes, chases follow-ups, handles review requests, and keeps customers, jobs, and schedule straight — so the paperwork stops eating your evenings. Nothing he drafts goes out without the owner’s approval.

Deals & inventory

Scout

“I only buzz when a deal is worth the drive.”

Scout watches stores, clearance, and listings around the clock — penny finds, price drops, resale-worthy tools and equipment — and keeps an eye on inventory so the shop knows what it has and what it needs.

C
Repair & maintenance

Cody

“Snap a photo — I’ll rank the likely causes.”

Cody fronts the Repair Guide. Send a photo of what’s broken and he ranks the likely causes, lays out the checks in the right order, and tells you plainly whether it’s a do-it-yourself fix or a shop job.

T
Money & payroll

Tally

“The books balance — or I’ll tell you why not.”

Tally owns the money screens: invoices, payments, the books, and payroll prep. She keeps the numbers straight and flags what does not add up — and payroll math is never touched by guesswork.

Site Scanner

Gage

“Measure twice. Quote once.”

Gage measures from photos using a reference object and sourced measurements, then checks the number against the field — so a quote starts from a real measurement instead of a guess.

Manufacturing

Mack

“You bring the idea. I’ll get it built.”

Mack fronts manufacturing and prototyping: fixtures, parts, and ideas that need to exist. He lines up the build with our partner shops. One rule he never breaks: Mack does not quote prices — every job is priced in writing before work starts.

Social

Piper

“Drafted, queued, and waiting on your yes.”

Piper prepares and proposes social posts — your finished work is your best advertising. She drafts and queues; nothing is ever published without the owner’s approval.
