## Purpose

Close the highest-value gaps identified when Highway 38 was compared with established quoting, field-service, construction-management, and business-operations platforms.

This work improves trust, buyer clarity, hands-on product proof, onboarding transparency, and production verification without changing approved prices, product IDs, subscription terms, customer records, authenticated deployment IDs, or owner-controlled external-action boundaries.

## Interactive demo boundary

The public quote demo:

- uses browser memory only;
- does not create an account;
- does not save a customer or quote;
- does not upload files;
- does not call H38 AI;
- does not send email or text;
- does not charge or request payment;
- does not submit information to Highway 38;
- is not a real customer quote or authorization to proceed.
