# New-House Demonstration — Permanent Scope Lock

This file is authoritative for the Universal Quote Builder whole-house demonstration.

## Project type

The demonstration is a **ground-up new-house construction project on an undeveloped lot**. It is not a renovation, remodel, existing-condition repair, or property-improvement project.

## Required construction sequence

1. Boundary/topographic verification, construction staking, permits, and mobilization
2. Erosion control, tree/brush clearing, grubbing, stripping, and debris handling
3. Temporary access, excavation, cut/fill, subgrade preparation, and dewatering if required
4. Underground utilities, well/septic or municipal connections, sleeves, and service coordination
5. Footings, foundation walls, waterproofing, drainage, insulation, backfill, and slab preparation
6. Floor, wall, and roof framing; sheathing; weatherproof shell; windows and exterior doors
7. Roofing, siding, soffit/fascia, decks/porches, garage, and exterior concrete
8. Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, fire/safety, low-voltage, and inspections
9. Insulation, drywall, interior doors/trim, cabinetry, countertops, flooring, painting, fixtures, and appliances
10. Final grading, drainage, driveway, walks, landscaping, testing, punch list, as-builts, and closeout

## CAD rules

- No floating symbols, fixtures, equipment, cabinets, ducts, pipes, panels, receptacles, lights, notes, or dimensions.
- Every object must be visibly anchored to a wall, floor, roof, grade line, foundation, equipment pad, utility route, cabinet run, or labeled reference grid.
- Mechanical, plumbing, and electrical sheets must use the architectural floor plan as a screened underlay with room names, walls, doors, and major fixed equipment.
- Site sheets must show clearing limits, access, erosion controls, stockpile/staging areas, proposed house footprint, utilities, drainage, and existing/proposed grades.
- Foundation and framing information must represent new construction rather than alterations to an existing structure.
- Drawings remain estimating/coordination documents until verified dimensions and required licensed-professional review are complete.

## Scope-drift prevention

Before changing the package, generator, drawings, quote definitions, verification, or public wording, confirm all of the following:

- The project is still described as a new house on a cleared lot.
- The first field phase is clearing/site preparation, not demolition.
- Quote phases follow ground-up construction order.
- Trade drawings retain an architectural underlay and have no floating objects.
- No renovation, existing-system modification, hidden-damage, selective-demolition, or repair language was introduced unless explicitly approved by Rick.

Any conflicting text or drawing is a defect and must block release.
