# Conversation & Meeting Assistant safety boundary

Meeting AI may organize, summarize, suggest, and prepare context.

It does not automatically approve or accept a quote, send a quote, send customer/vendor messages, promise pricing, commit Highway 38 to work, schedule work, purchase anything, pay anything, or create a financial obligation.

The meeting system also does not auto-start recording. Audio begins only from an intentional Start recording / Start Visit Assistant action with visible recording state. Recollections remain labeled as recollections, and unverified dimensions remain unverified downstream.
