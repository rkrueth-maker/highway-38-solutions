Universal Smart Import

Bring the spreadsheets you already have.

Business Office can inspect CSV, JSON, XLS, and XLSX files and identify likely business records instead of treating every spreadsheet as a dead attachment.

Detect the record type

Workbook sheets can be classified as Customers, Properties, Jobs, Quotes, Invoices, Time, Expenses, Payments, or historical records based on the data actually present.

Match before creating

Existing customers can be compared by IDs, email, phone, address, name, and company. Existing nonblank values are protected and conflicts are surfaced instead of silently overwritten.

Stage, review, then apply

Each detected group gets a separate staging run. Low-confidence data is not auto-selected, and nothing is committed until the owner reviews and applies the staged group.

Import does not create external authority. A spreadsheet import cannot automatically send an invoice, contact a customer, schedule work, buy anything, move money, or approve a customer-facing action.
