Important boundary

Planning support does not replace licensed or field verification.

Public examples and concept documents are planning demonstrations. Final dimensions, site conditions, code requirements, structural design, electrical work, plumbing work, permits, stamped documents, and regulated professional decisions must be verified by the appropriate qualified person before construction or release.

Human-controlled work

AI may help organize, compare, draft, visualize, and identify missing details. Highway 38 does not treat unverified AI output as an approved price, field dimension, code requirement, engineering decision, customer commitment, or authorization to proceed.
